# Surface: home (index.html)

Scope: the single-page portfolio at ncgg.dev. Visitor mode: Experience.
Audience/job: reviewers, recruiters, teammates arriving from a link; learn the disciplines, see work, make contact (email, GitHub).
Constraints: placeholders for projects and contact, clearly labelled. Motion pausable, reduced-motion respected.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a live ASCII fluid, and the name, sections and media are made of or float on it. Refuses the category default of a hero headline plus project card grid.
OWN-WORLD: Pure #000 ground (default; an opt-in light theme redefines the same tokens). Gray ASCII glyph field (dots, dashes, tildes, directional strokes) at 15-60% luminance; text in near-white and mid gray. Monospace only: Martian Mono (variable weight/width, display) and Azeret Mono (body). Hairline 1px rules in dark gray. Progressive backdrop blur bands at viewport top and bottom and behind text columns. No colour, no cards, no shadows.
STORY: Visitor stirs the field, reads "ncgg" formed in glyphs, understands a maker spanning CAD, software, games, 3D, AI and robotics, scans staggered work slots with generative ASCII media, sees languages, emails or opens GitHub.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed fluid canvas. "ncgg" rendered inside the field as dense glyphs, ~60% viewport width, left-of-centre. Corners: top-left wordmark, top-right nav + motion toggle; bottom-left one-line descriptor; bottom-right live cursor/velocity readout. Primary action: nav "contact".
FORM: seed: none (pinned) — "ASCII fluid simulations and waves in gray against a completely black background. Make it react to the cursor's movements." "Use monospace fonts, and creative placing of text and media." "Use gradual blurring". Signature interaction: cursor/scroll/click inject velocity and density; the glyph name smears and springs back. Motion grammar: unblur-on-enter reveals, per-glyph weight proximity on headings, text scramble on nav.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
