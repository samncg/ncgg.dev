# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step and no dependencies (user's choice). Deployable as-is to any static host.

## Users

People evaluating ncgg as a maker: recruiters, internship and university programme reviewers, robotics teammates and mentors, and collaborators who arrive from a link (CV, GitHub, competition contacts) and want to know quickly what ncgg builds and how to get in touch.

## Product Purpose

ncgg.dev is the personal portfolio of ncgg, a university student. It exists to show breadth across making disciplines and to give a way to make contact. Success: a visitor leaves knowing the disciplines, has seen project work, and can reach ncgg by email or GitHub.

## Positioning

A maker whose work spans the physical and the digital: CAD design, 3D modelling, game development, software, and competition robotics. The site itself is a working piece of software, an interactive simulation, rather than a template.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single page portfolio: intro, disciplines, project work, languages, contact.
- Confirmed disciplines: CAD design, software, game development, 3D modelling, AI, electronics/PCB design, 3D printing ("and more").
- Light mode exists as an opt-in; dark stays the default identity.
- Confirmed AI experience: ComfyUI, local language models, AI workflows, agentic AI, and machine-learning knowledge.
- Confirmed competitions: FTC (FIRST Tech Challenge) and NRC.
- Confirmed languages: Java, C#, Python; currently learning Rust.
- Contact channels: GitHub (samncg) and email (address not yet supplied).

## Brand Commitments

- Name/domain: ncgg.dev.
- Binding visual constraints from the user: monochrome; ASCII fluid simulation and waves in gray on a completely black background; reacts to cursor movement; clean and minimal yet interactive; monospace fonts; creative placement of text and media; motion that reacts to the user; gradual (progressive) blur.

## Product Principles

1. The site demonstrates craft; it does not describe it.
2. Breadth is the story: physical and digital making shown side by side.
3. Every interaction answers the visitor's own movement.
4. Never claim what has not been confirmed; placeholders are labelled as placeholders.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Motion must be pausable (continuous animation) and respect prefers-reduced-motion; text stays readable over the animated field.
